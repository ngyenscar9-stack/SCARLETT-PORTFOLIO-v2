The idea

RU — The Rhythm of Rest is a pre-arrival sleep profile for voco Scenia Bay Nha Trang by IHG. Guests configure rest preferences before arrival so the hotel can prepare elements such as pillow, scent, tea, room temperature and sound before the guest enters the room.

Why RU works

01 — Remove effort from the guest

The project reframed personalisation around lower effort, not simply wider choice. Instead of asking guests to request comfort items during the stay, RU moves the preference capture before arrival.

02 — Turn amenities into a journey

The experience runs from Sleep Profile → room preparation → preferences already in place → sensory wind-down → better-rest experience.

03 — Make it ownable

The proposal connects personalisation, restorative rest and local Nha Trang character into a repeatable signature experience rather than a one-off amenity list.

04 — Make it operational

The implementation plan moves from building the system and pilot to launch, guest feedback, optimisation and repeat-stay recognition.
