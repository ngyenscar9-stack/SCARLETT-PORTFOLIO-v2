HopeBox

A Business Solution Design Report focused on one core challenge: how HopeBox could build more consistent corporate engagement beyond seasonal peaks and create a stronger year-round B2B relationship system rather than relying only on campaign spikes.

01 / Business Problem

Revenue was not the whole problem.

HopeBox operates with year-round programme and support costs, while corporate purchasing and engagement are concentrated around Tet, Mid-Autumn and Christmas. The project reframed that instability as a relationship problem: corporate partners had limited reasons and structures to stay engaged between seasonal peaks.

Strategic shift

From seasonal spikes to continuous touchpoints.

Instead of treating the issue as a short-term sales dip, the team focused on building continuous corporate touchpoints that could support partnership continuity, stronger brand engagement and more stable revenue logic across the full year.

02 / Stakeholder Insight

Prioritise the people who shape continuity.

Corporate B2B partners were prioritised because procurement decisions, CSR budgets and long-term partnership commitment directly affect HopeBox's revenue continuity. Stakeholder mapping and empathy work highlighted a clear need for measurable impact, clearer engagement opportunities and stronger year-round visibility.

03 / Decision Logic

Four routes. One practical quick win.

The ideation stage produced four solution routes across continuous engagement and strategic partnership. An Action Priority Matrix was then used to compare implementation effort with strategic impact. The Mid-Year Impact Gala emerged as the strongest quick-win direction.

Why this route

Why the gala?

It creates a dedicated mid-year reason for corporate partners to reconnect with HopeBox during a low-engagement period, while combining fundraising, networking, impact storytelling and public recognition in one experience.

What it unlocks

More than a one-off event.

The concept opens room for deeper ESG conversations, sponsorship pathways and post-event communication that can continue beyond the physical activation itself.

04 / Prototype System

A concept built across the full partner journey.

The proposal was translated into a connected prototype system rather than a single event visual. The artefacts were designed to cover the gala experience, partnership collateral, impact storytelling and post-event digital recognition as one coherent B2B communication journey.

05 / Outcome & Assessment

A structured proposal with a solid result.

The final stage defined how the concept should be evaluated through desirability, viability and feasibility. Alongside the design process itself, the report received an 82 / 100 result for Assignment 3 — Business Solution Design Report.

01

Desirability

RSVP intention, sponsor interest, storytelling response and audience feedback indicate whether the experience is meaningful enough to attract both corporate and community engagement.

02

Viability

Sponsorship uptake, donation conversion, fundraising efficiency and repeat partnership interest indicate whether the concept can support more sustainable revenue behaviour.

03

Feasibility

Logistics, staff coordination, production requirements and low-resource digital follow-up indicate whether HopeBox could realistically deliver and repeat the model.
