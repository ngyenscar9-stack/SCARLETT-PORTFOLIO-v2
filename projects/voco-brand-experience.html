01 / BUSINESS CHALLENGE

A crowded category needed stronger memory.

voco Scenia Bay Nha Trang operates in a competitive coastal hotel market where beachfront facilities, ocean views and premium hospitality are increasingly easy for travellers to compare.

The project focused on a brand-awareness problem: making the voco name more distinctive and creating a stronger association between the hotel and the Nha Trang experience.

The brand problem

Generic hotel content made it difficult for a relatively new property to build strong top-of-mind recognition against established competitors.

The opportunity

Travellers increasingly use digital and AI-supported touchpoints during destination discovery, creating room for more memorable pre-arrival brand experiences.

02 / STRATEGIC DIRECTION

Make Nha Trang easier to remember through voco.

The selected direction uses AI personalisation and immersive technology to address two connected weaknesses: low brand awareness and undifferentiated digital content.

Instead of promoting another list of hotel features, the strategy creates two complementary experiences that strengthen emotional engagement and tangible understanding before arrival.

03 / TWO DIGITAL EXPERIENCES

Two ideas. One pre-arrival memory.

The two recommendations were designed to complement rather than replace each other: one builds emotional and social connection, while the other makes the physical hotel experience easier to understand before booking.

01

If Nha Trang Had a Voice

A personalised destination storytelling experience where travellers choose their travel mood, companions and preferences. The system then produces a shareable “Your Nha Trang” digital postcard using a controlled, brand-approved content library.

Interactive 360° Virtual Tour

04 / IMPLEMENTATION

From concept to a 12-month rollout.

The recommendations were developed with implementation and feasibility in mind, moving from content preparation and production through launch, measurement and optimisation.

Q1 Planning & Development Define segments, approve scripts and content libraries, prepare the technical build and production requirements.
Q2 Production & Testing Produce destination assets, capture the property experience and test both digital concepts before launch.
Q3 Launch & Promotion Launch the two experiences and connect them to the hotel website, social content and campaign touchpoints.
Q4 Evaluation & Optimisation Monitor engagement, sharing, branded search and commercial indicators, then optimise the experience.
05 / PROFESSIONAL COMMUNICATION

Strategy, then make it persuasive.

Alongside the written Business Improvement Report, the team translated the proposal into a professional role-play presentation designed to communicate the problem, recommendations and strategic rationale in a more engaging client-facing format.
